# Tone & Voice Guide

A guide to establishing an authentic, persuasive, and professional technical voice while eliminating artificial AI markers and buzzwords.

---

## Core Tone Principles

1. **Confident, Not Arrogant:** Present facts and outcomes objectively. Use active verbs without exaggerated self-praise (*"Engineered"* instead of *"Single-handedly revolutionized"*).
2. **Specific, Not Vague:** Mention exact frameworks, libraries, and quantitative outcomes instead of vague generalizations (*"built a backend service"* vs *"developed a Python microservice with FastAPI"*).
3. **Concise & Direct:** Limit each bullet to 1–2 lines maximum. Focus strictly on the technical achievement and business value.

---

## Banned AI Buzzwords & Substitutes

Basic AI models often overuse generic buzzwords that raise red flags for recruiters. Replace them with precise technical terms:

| Banned AI Buzzword | Why It Fails | Recommended Technical Alternative |
|---|---|---|
| **Leveraged** | Overused, vague corporate jargon | *Utilized*, *Applied*, *Integrated*, *Implemented* |
| **Spearheaded** | Overly dramatic for technical tasks | *Led*, *Architected*, *Initiated*, *Directed* |
| **Utilized** | Stilted and formal | *Used*, *Deployed*, *Configured*, *Applied* |
| **Synergy / Seamless** | Empty marketing buzzwords | *Interoperable*, *Synchronized*, *Integrated* |
| **Pivotal / Groundbreaking** | Subjective self-praise | Let metrics prove impact (*"reduced latency by 40%"*) |
| **Passionate about** | Cliché filler text | Demonstrate passion through projects and results |

## Calibrating Tone by Seniority Level

Match the vocabulary and perspective to the target role level:

### 1. Student / Intern Level
- **Focus:** Hands-on execution, rapid learning, project completion, foundational skills.
- **Key Verbs:** *Developed*, *Built*, *Configured*, *Implemented*, *Analyzed*, *Created*.
- **Avoid:** Claiming executive authority or enterprise strategy (*"drove company-wide digital transformation"*).

### 2. Junior to Mid-Level Engineer
- **Focus:** Autonomy, feature ownership, optimization, collaboration, code quality.
- **Key Verbs:** *Architected*, *Optimized*, *Refactored*, *Automated*, *Integrated*, *Streamlined*.

---

## Handling Metrics & Honest Estimations

Never invent metrics out of thin air, but always quantify impact using realistic engineering benchmarks:

- **Estimating Scale:** If a project ran locally or in a lab environment, quantify the input dataset size or request rate (e.g., *"tested against 5,000 synthetic requests"*).
- **Estimating Time Savings:** If automation replaced a manual step, estimate weekly time saved (e.g., *"saving ~4 hours of weekly manual testing"*).
- **Using Approximations:** Use `~`, `>`, or `+` to signal honest estimates (e.g., `~15% performance gain`, `100+ test cases`).
